## Recommendation

Don't build city or state pages. The competitor pages are one template with the place name swapped; copying them would add about 120 near-identical pages with nothing locally useful, and could suggest offices we don't have.

Instead, once the facts are confirmed:

1. **Website development: one "Where we work" section** on `/website-development` (and a line on `/contact`). It would list the countries we take clients in, say plainly that the work is remote, and give the time zones and currency we work in. A country page is only worth it where the work really differs and we've done it, for example Arabic and right-to-left layouts or local payment providers for Gulf clients. No Charleston neighbourhood pages without a real Charleston presence.

2. **Wholesale: one "Where we ship" section** on `/wholesale/ordering` listing the states and countries we ship to and exclude, carriers if they're fixed, and any destination with its own restriction. Move it to a `/wholesale/shipping` page only if the list and rules grow. No per-state pages unless a state's rules really differ, and those need a compliance review first.

3. **Structured data:** set `areaServed` to the confirmed coverage. It says "US" now (since before this PR) and isn't confirmed.

Owner questions that unlock this:

- Which countries do you take website clients in, remotely? Any currency, contract or time-zone limits?
- Do you meet website clients in person anywhere? Is there an office address you want published?
- Which US states and which countries do you ship wholesale orders to, and which do you exclude?
- Where do wholesale orders ship from? (This is separate from where products are made.)
